# IntelliJ IDEA — Proscan Plugin

## Install

1. Open IntelliJ IDEA
2. Go to Settings > Plugins > Marketplace
3. Search for **Proscan**
4. Click **Install** and restart the IDE

## Configure

1. Go to Settings > Tools > Proscan
2. Enter your **Server URL**
3. Enter your **API Token**
4. Click **Test Connection** to verify
5. Click **Apply**

## Usage

After configuration, findings from your Proscan scans appear as editor inspections. They show up in the gutter, in the inspection results panel, and as highlights in the code.

**Features:**
- Gutter icons indicating finding severity
- Inspection details with CWE, description, and remediation guidance
- Quick-fix intentions for common vulnerability patterns
- Trigger scans from the Tools menu or the toolbar

## Compatibility

The plugin works with IntelliJ IDEA, WebStorm, PyCharm, GoLand, and other JetBrains IDEs built on the IntelliJ platform.
